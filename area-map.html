Reading Disability Documentation

What a report can establish, and what it cannot

Accommodation in practiceAccessibilityPractised · resource in developmentWell established

A psychoeducational or medical report records a person’s performance on particular instruments at one point in time. An accommodation decision concerns the barriers that person meets in a particular course. The challenge is to read the first without asking it to answer the second by itself. This resource covers what standard scores, percentiles and confidence intervals do and do not establish; the distinction between a diagnosis, a functional limitation and a barrier; the weight professional guidance gives to a student’s own account; and the limits of a clinician’s recommendation as evidence. The reading of an individual file, and the decision that follows from it, rest with the accommodation office.

Grounded in Standards for Educational and Psychological Testing (AERA, APA & NCME); validity theory (Messick); AHEAD documentation guidance; ICF (WHO, 2001)

Trauma-informed, developmental and student-centred

Accommodation in practiceAccessibilityPractised · resource in developmentWell established

A conversation about access asks a student to describe a disability to someone who holds authority over the outcome. Disability Justice and Where It Came From

A framework with named authors, and where to read them

GroundingAccessibilityPractised · resource in developmentWell established

Patty Berne, Stacey Park Milbern, Mia Mingus, Leroy F. Moore Jr., Eli Clare and Sebastian Margaret are among its architects, and the ten principles were set out by Berne and Sins Invalid. Assessment Access and Validity Review

Engagement · Accessible Learning Labs

EngagementsOffered as an engagementWell established

For programmes, testing centres and credentialing bodies. Assessments are examined for construct-irrelevant variance: the time limits, formats and conditions that measure something other than the outcome. Conformance and the duty to accommodate set the floor; the people who take the assessment take part in judging what it measures; and evaluation is built into the redesign so the assessment keeps improving. The result reduces how often individual accommodation is needed, without treating accommodation as lesser, and leaves the assessment more valid for everyone who takes it.

Grounded in Educational measurement; validity theory; Universal Design for Learning; accommodation law

Learning and Training Commitments Review

For organisations required to publish an accessibility plan under the Accessible British Columbia Act or the Accessible Canada Act. Every plan commits to learning: staff training, faculty development, accessible onboarding, mandatory courses. Those commitments are where a plan changes practice or does not.
